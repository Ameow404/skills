## Decision criteria (used when the workflow surfaces opportunities)
1. **Spec ownership / correctness definition** — does the work let you own the definition of
   what counts as correct (evaluation, standards, verification)?
2. **Asset accumulation** — does it accumulate data, data access rights, or social capital
   that is hard to replicate?
3. **Bottleneck position** — is it at a place where the field's bottleneck is moving?
4. **Leverage** — can you do it without permission from a single gatekeeper (compute, data,
   distribution)? Prefer work whose value does not depend on correctly predicting which
   model lab wins.
5. **Defensibility** — would the position survive being copied by a better-resourced actor?
